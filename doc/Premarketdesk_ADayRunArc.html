PremarketDesk architecture

A Day in PremarketDesk

There is no server and no queue. The whole system is five Windows Task Scheduler jobs on one machine, each waking at its clock time, reading the files earlier jobs left behind, and leaving files for the next. The architecture is the clock.

The weekday, end to end

Five jobs, one timeline

Every weekday runs itself. Discovery picks the morning's gappers, the collector listens to live trades, the 08:45 chain turns evidence into an emailed report, and the night jobs, shown in amber here and throughout this page, come back at 22:15 to write down what was really true. Sunday evening a sixth pass rebuilds the discovery universe for the coming week.

07:15 to 08:55

The morning pipeline: files passed hand to hand

Each stage writes one artifact and the next stage reads it. Nothing talks to anything else at runtime; if a stage dies, its file is simply missing and the next stage says so in the packet's gaps_to_fill instead of crashing. Every market number in every stage comes from EODHD, over REST or the trades websocket. One handoff is special: the collector is still appending to its bar file when scan reads it at 08:45, so scan freezes a snapshot copy at runs/<date>/premarket_snapshot.jsonl, parses only complete lines, and discards a partial trailing line, recording what it saw in the packet. And after deliver, a last step rebuilds site/PremarketDesk.html, the double click archive of every morning's report.

22:15, the part that happens after dark

Where the backfill happens, and why it exists at all

Two facts force the backfill to exist. First, the premarket session opens at 04:00 ET but the collector only starts listening at 07:20, so the morning's premarket high, low, and VWAP honestly describe a partial window. Second, EODHD publishes its one minute intraday bars a few hours behind live, so the full 04:00 to 09:30 truth simply does not exist anywhere until the evening.

So the day's numbers are written twice. At 08:45 the scan records what the collector actually heard, labelled partial where it was partial. At 22:15 the backfill fetches the now published intraday bars and writes pm_high_true, pm_low_true, and pm_vwap_true into the same picks rows, next to the morning values, never over them. The gap between the two column families is itself a measurement: it is exactly what starting the collector at 07:20 costs.

Where, concretely, does it run?

  1. Task Scheduler fires PremarketDesk\nightly at 22:15 on weekdays.
  2. That runs tasks\job_nightly.bat, which runs src\backfill_premarket.py, then src\fill_outcomes.py, then src\build_archive.py on this machine.
  3. Backfill writes the true premarket columns into the picks table of data\premarketdesk.db, and while the intraday feed is open it also runs the definitive collector volume check, saved to runs\<date>\verify_intraday.json.
  4. Everything it prints lands in logs\nightly-<date>.log.

One picks row, filled in three passes

The clearest way to see the flow is to follow a single database row. The scan creates it in the morning, the backfill widens it the same night, and the outcomes pass returns one and five trading sessions later to record what the market actually did next. Those outcome rows are the project's long game: the CRITERIA.md thresholds are unvalidated seed values until enough filled rows say otherwise. And the sample unit for that judgment is the session, not the row: twelve candidates from one morning share the same market, the same macro print, and often the same sector move, so they are one observation, not twelve. Any later analysis aggregates to one figure per session before comparing conviction buckets, and fill_outcomes.py reports both counts to keep that distinction visible.

Degradation, job by job

When something fails

The chain stops at the first failure, evidence gaps degrade to nulls with written reasons, and staleness is refused loudly: any job reading universe.json exits and names the file's age once it passes ten days. While the data/UNVERIFIED gate marker exists, no email goes out in any of these rows regardless of what else succeeded.

a market holiday arrives Every weekday job runs market_today.py first. On a weekend or an official exchange holiday (cached from EODHD exchange-details, refreshed weekly) the job logs one line and exits cleanly: no watchlist from stale quotes, no empty collection, no report about a session that does not exist. If the calendar is unreachable and no cache exists, the guard assumes the market is open, because a false closed silently loses a real morning.
universe.py fails Sunday The week runs on the last good universe.json until it turns ten days old, then discover and scan refuse to run, printing the age and the limit. No packet, no report, no email past that point.
discover.py fails at 07:15 No fresh watchlist and no warmed baselines. The collector exits naming the missing file (or runs on yesterday's list if one is on disk). Scan still builds candidates from its own fresh bulk call, marks them collector uncovered with RVOL null and reasons. The report goes out with the holes named in the disclaimer.
collector never connects No minute bars. Every premarket high, low, VWAP, and RVOL is null with a reason, gaps_to_fill names every candidate. The report goes out, thin and saying so.
scan.py fails at 08:45 No packet, so the chain stops before the model. No report, no email; the morning log ends at the scan step's exit code.
analyst.py fails or times out Two attempts at 218 seconds each, three times the slowest of five measured runs, then the deterministic fallback: report.md is rebuilt as plain tables straight from packet.json, the disclaimer carries the failure reason, analyst_usage.json records status timeout or failed. Render and deliver carry on, the email still goes.
deliver.py fails The report is on disk in runs/<date>/ and the log holds Resend's answer. No email that morning and no morning archive rebuild, but the evening rebuild still catches the day; nothing else retries until the next scheduled morning.
build_archive.py fails site/PremarketDesk.html keeps its previous build. Every other artifact of the day is already on disk, and the next rebuild, evening or morning, replaces the file whole because every build is a full rebuild.
nightly jobs fail The true premarket columns and outcomes stay null. Both jobs are idempotent and fill only what is missing, so the next night's run picks up the backlog on its own. The morning report is unaffected.

Load bearing rules

Five decisions that shape everything above

EODHD is the only market data source.
REST for quotes, history, news, calendars; the websocket for live trades. No second vendor, ever.
Every threshold lives in doc/CRITERIA.md.
No Python file contains a screening number. criteria.py is a strict reader that raises on anything missing, so a threshold cannot quietly exist in two places.
Python decides, the model narrates.
Membership, eligibility, scores, and conviction are computed deterministically before analyst.py pipes the packet to the claude CLI. A containment check fails the run if the report mentions a ticker the packet does not carry.
Missing evidence is null, with a written reason.
A premarket RVOL that cannot be computed honestly is null plus a reason string, never a substituted guess. The report's disclaimer line names every such candidate.
Email is gated until a human verifies a real morning.
While data/UNVERIFIED exists, deliver.py refuses to send no matter what keys are configured. Only a human deletes that file.
